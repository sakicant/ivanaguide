Krka National Park Private Walking Tour

Skradinski Buk Experience

Experience the natural beauty of Krka National Park on a private walking tour through Skradinski Buk. Explore cascading waterfalls, crystal-clear river landscapes, and Mediterranean vegetation while discovering the unique ecosystem, traditional watermills, and the centuries-old connection between the Krka River and the local community through authentic stories and local insights.

Please note: This experience includes only the private guided walking tour within Krka National Park (Skradinski Buk area). Transportation to and from the park, private transfers, and entrance tickets are not included.

Guests may purchase their entrance ticket online in advance, or the guide can assist with the purchase upon arrival. Together, the guide and guests will take the official Krka National Park shuttle bus or boat to Skradinski Buk, where the private guided tour begins.

Choose Your Skradinski Buk Experience

Option 1: Lozovac Entrance & Exit

180€ / group

The Most Convenient Option

Start your visit at the Lozovac entrance, where your guide will meet you before entering the park.

From the parking area, a Krka National Park shuttle bus (free) takes visitors approximately 7 minutes to reach the Skradinski Buk waterfalls area. After arriving at the waterfalls, your private walking tour begins.

During the walk, you will explore the wooden pathways, viewpoints, historic watermills, and natural surroundings while learning about the river ecosystem and local traditions.

After the tour, you return to the Lozovac entrance using the park shuttle bus.

Route: Lozovac entrance → park shuttle bus (approx. 7 minutes) → Skradinski Buk → guided walking tour → return by shuttle bus to Lozovac

Option 2: Skradin Entrance & Exit

220€ / group

A Scenic Boat Arrival Experience

Begin your visit from the charming riverside town of Skradin. From here, you will take the official Krka National Park boat ride to Skradinski Buk.

The boat journey takes approximately 20 minutes each way, offering beautiful views of the Krka River and the surrounding landscape.

After arriving at Skradinski Buk, your private walking tour begins. Explore the waterfalls, wooden paths, historic mills, and natural beauty of the park with your local guide.

After the tour, you return by boat from Skradinski Buk back to Skradin.

Route: Skradin entrance → park boat (approx. 20 minutes) → Skradinski Buk → guided walking tour → return boat to Skradin

Option 3: Lozovac Entrance & Skradin Exit

200€ / group

The Best Combination of Convenience & Scenery

This option combines the easiest arrival with a scenic departure. It is the ideal choice for guests arriving by taxi, as it allows you to experience both entrances to the park without worrying about returning to your vehicle.

Please note: This option is not recommended for guests arriving by private car, as the tour ends in Skradin while your vehicle remains at the Lozovac entrance, making the return to the starting point less convenient.

Start at the Lozovac entrance, where you will take the Krka National Park shuttle bus from the parking area to Skradinski Buk (approximately 7 minutes).

After meeting your guide, you will explore the waterfalls and surrounding area on a private walking tour, discovering the natural and cultural highlights of Skradinski Buk.

After the guided tour, you continue your visit independently and take the official Krka National Park boat from Skradinski Buk to Skradin. The boat ride takes approximately 20 minutes and offers a beautiful final view of the Krka River landscape.

Route: Lozovac entrance → park shuttle bus (approx. 7 minutes) → Skradinski Buk → guided walking tour → independent boat ride to Skradin
