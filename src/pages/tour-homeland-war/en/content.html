Homeland War Experience

Discover the city's recent history through the personal memories of a local guide who experienced the Croatian Homeland War as a child

Explore one of the defining chapters of modern Croatian history on a private walking tour that tells the story of Šibenik during the Croatian Homeland War (1991–1995). This is more than a history tour. It is a personal journey through the memories of a local guide who experienced the war as a child.

As you walk through the historic Old Town, you will discover how this centuries-old city faced one of the most challenging periods in its modern history, how its citizens defended their homes, and how Šibenik preserved its strength and identity during the conflict. Alongside important historical events, including the famous September Battle of 1991, your guide shares her own childhood memories of growing up during the war, offering a rare and personal perspective on everyday life in Šibenik during those years.

This fully private experience is conducted at your pace. By combining historical facts with first-hand memories, this tour offers an authentic understanding of how the war shaped not only Šibenik, but also the lives of the people who called this city home.

Highlights of the Šibenik Homeland War Private Walking Tour Experience

  • Šibenik Old Town - discover how daily life continued in the historic centre during the war and hear personal stories about living through uncertain times
  • Cathedral of St. James - learn about the importance of this UNESCO-listed masterpiece as a symbol of Šibenik's cultural identity and resilience. Please note that entrance tickets to the cathedral are not included in the tour price.
  • Šibenik Waterfront - understand the strategic importance of the coastline and harbour during the defence of the city while hearing stories connected to this important area
  • September 1991 Battle of Šibenik - discover the story of one of the key events in the defence of Šibenik and its role in the Croatian Homeland War
  • Šibenik Fortresses - learn about the historical defensive role of the city's fortresses and their symbolic importance during the Homeland War while viewing them from the Old Town. Please note that this tour does not include visits to the fortresses themselves.
  • Life During the War - gain insight into how local families, children, and communities adapted to wartime conditions and how the city continued to live, work, and rebuild

About Your Guide

Your guide is a licensed local expert from Šibenik, officially certified by the Croatian Ministry of Tourism. Born and raised in Šibenik, she offers a unique perspective that combines professional guiding experience with personal memories of living through the Croatian Homeland War.

As a child during the war, she experienced the uncertainty, changes, and emotions that shaped everyday life in Šibenik during the early 1990s. Through her own memories and stories shared by family, neighbours, and the local community, she brings a human perspective to this important period of history.

Her approach is not only about military events and historical dates, but about the people behind the history - families, children, and citizens who continued their lives during challenging times. With respect and sensitivity, she creates a meaningful experience that helps visitors understand both the events and their impact on the local community.

This is more than a history tour. It is an opportunity to hear Šibenik's recent history from someone whose own life story is connected to it.
