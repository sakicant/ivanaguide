Šibenik Homeland War Experience

A Story Told Through Personal Memories

Explore one of the most important chapters of modern Croatian history on a private walking tour through Šibenik’s recent past. This unique experience combines historical events with personal memories from a local guide who lived through the Croatian Homeland War as a child.

Walk through the Old Town and discover how Šibenik faced the challenges of the 1990s, how its citizens defended their city, and how the community preserved its identity during difficult times. Learn about key events such as the September 1991 Battle of Šibenik while hearing personal stories that bring history closer and reveal the human side of the conflict.

Why Choose This Private Tour?

  • Private experience tailored to your interests and pace
  • Led by a licensed local guide from Šibenik
  • Personal memories and authentic local perspective
  • Understand the Croatian Homeland War beyond dates and facts
  • Ideal for travelers seeking authentic stories

Tour Highlights

  • Šibenik Old Town and stories of everyday life during the war
  • Cathedral of St. James and its cultural significance (entrance not included)
  • Šibenik Waterfront and its strategic importance
  • September 1991 Battle of Šibenik
  • Stories of Šibenik’s fortresses and their historical role (fortress visits not included)
  • Personal stories about life in Šibenik during the war years
