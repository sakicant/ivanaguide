Private Šibenik Family Walking Tour

Discover Šibenik Together Through the Eyes of a Family-Friendly Local Guide

Create unforgettable family memories in Šibenik with a private walking tour designed for both children and adults. Discover the city's historic Old Town through engaging stories, fun discoveries, and interactive moments that transform history into an experience the whole family can enjoy.

Led by a licensed local guide from Šibenik, this tour goes beyond traditional sightseeing. Instead of focusing on dates and facts, you’ll uncover local legends, hidden details, and fascinating stories that bring the city’s past to life for every generation.

Why Choose This Private Family Tour?

  • Private experience tailored to your family’s interests and pace
  • Led by a licensed local guide with authentic local knowledge
  • Family-friendly storytelling adapted for children and adults
  • Perfect for families, multi-generational groups, and cruise guests

Tour Highlights

  • UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James (entrance not included)
  • Šibenik Old Town with charming stone streets and hidden corners
  • St. Barbara Church and its unique clock
  • Šibenik City Hall and main square
  • Stories of Šibenik's medieval history, battles, and fortresses (fortress visits not included)

What's Included & Not Included
