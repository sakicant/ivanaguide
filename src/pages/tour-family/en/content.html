Family Private Walking Tour

Discover Šibenik Together Through the Eyes of a Family-Friendly Local Guide

Turn your visit to Šibenik into a memorable family adventure on a private walking tour designed especially for parents and children. Instead of a traditional sightseeing tour filled with dates and facts, this experience brings the city's history to life through engaging stories, fun discoveries, and interactive moments that keep younger visitors curious while adults enjoy the rich heritage of Šibenik.

As you stroll through the charming stone streets of the Old Town, you'll discover medieval squares, hidden alleys, and impressive landmarks, including the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James. Along the way, your licensed local guide shares fascinating legends, local traditions, and stories adapted to the ages and interests of your family, creating an enjoyable experience for everyone.

Because this is a fully private tour, the pace is completely flexible. There is always time for breaks, photos, questions, and spontaneous moments, making it a relaxed and stress-free way to explore Šibenik with children.

Highlights of the Šibenik Family Private Walking Tour Experience

  • Cathedral of St. James - discover the famous UNESCO World Heritage Site through fascinating stories suitable for both children and adults. Please note that entrance tickets to the cathedral are not included in the tour price.
  • Šibenik Waterfront - enjoy the lively seaside promenade while observing local life and beautiful coastal views
  • Šibenik Old Town - wander through narrow stone streets, hidden passages, and picturesque squares full of history and surprises
  • St. Barbara Church - discover the church with Šibenik's most unusual clock
  • Šibenik City Hall - admire the elegant Renaissance architecture overlooking the city's main square
  • Šibenik Fortresses - hear exciting stories about knights, battles, and the hilltop fortresses that once protected the city while admiring them from the Old Town. Please note that this tour does not include visits to the fortresses themselves.

About Your Guide

Your guide is a licensed local expert from Šibenik, officially certified by the Croatian Ministry of Tourism. Born and raised in Šibenik, she shares authentic local knowledge, personal stories, and insider perspectives that bring the city to life.

In addition to years of experience leading private tours, she previously worked in a school, giving her extensive experience working with children of different ages. She understands how to keep young visitors engaged through storytelling, conversation, and interactive learning, while ensuring adults enjoy meaningful cultural insights at the same time.

Every family is different, which is why each tour is adapted to your children's interests, energy levels, and pace. The goal is to create an enjoyable, educational, and memorable experience where both children and adults leave with wonderful memories of Šibenik.
