Private Šibenik History & Coffee with a Local Guide

Explore Šibenik's Old Town with a Local Guide and Enjoy an Authentic Coffee Experience

Discover Šibenik beyond the guidebooks on a private walking tour that combines history, culture, and a relaxed coffee conversation with a local guide. Explore the Old Town’s stone streets, squares, and iconic landmarks while hearing stories, traditions, and personal insights that reveal the true character of the city.

Led by a licensed local guide born and raised in Šibenik, this experience goes beyond traditional sightseeing. Discover the stories behind landmarks such as the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James, learn about Venetian heritage, and connect with Šibenik through the eyes of a local.

Finish your walk with a coffee at a local café, where you can ask your guide questions and receive authentic recommendations for places, local experiences, and everyday life in Šibenik.

  • Coffee with a local and personalized recommendations
