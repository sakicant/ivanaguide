History & Coffee with a Local Guide

Explore Šibenik's Old Town with a Local Guide and Enjoy an Authentic Coffee Experience

Experience Šibenik through a unique private walking tour that combines the city's rich history with a relaxed coffee conversation with a local guide. This experience offers more than a traditional sightseeing tour. It is a journey through centuries of history, followed by a personal moment to connect with your guide and discover the everyday life of Šibenik.

Explore the historic heart of Šibenik, from its medieval stone streets and Renaissance squares to its iconic landmarks and hidden corners. Led by a licensed local guide, you will discover the stories behind the city's most important sights, including the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James, while learning about Šibenik's Venetian heritage, traditions, and unique character.

After exploring the Old Town, take a break at a local café and enjoy a coffee while continuing the conversation in a relaxed atmosphere. This is your opportunity to ask questions, hear personal stories, and receive authentic recommendations about local food, hidden places, beaches, and experiences that only a local can share.

Designed for travellers who want to understand Šibenik beyond the guidebooks, this private experience combines history, culture, and genuine local connection - allowing you to experience the city through the eyes of someone who calls it home.

Highlights of Šibenik History & Coffee with a Local Guide Experience

  • Cathedral of St. James - UNESCO-listed Renaissance masterpiece and one of Croatia's most impressive architectural landmarks. Please note that entrance tickets to the cathedral are not included in the tour price.
  • Šibenik Old Town - explore a maze of stone streets, historic squares, and authentic Dalmatian atmosphere
  • Šibenik City Hall - admire the elegant Renaissance architecture surrounding the city's main square
  • St. Barbara Church - discover the church with Šibenik's most unusual clock, said to be the city's hardest clock to read
  • Šibenik Fortresses - hear about the defensive role of the city's fortresses throughout history, including their importance during the Homeland War, while viewing them from the Old Town. Please note that this tour does not include visits to the fortresses themselves.
  • Venetian heritage & medieval history - learn how centuries of cultural influences shaped the city
  • Local coffee experience - relax at a traditional café while discussing Šibenik, Croatian culture, and everyday local life
  • Hidden stories & local recommendations - discover places, traditions, and experiences you won't find in standard guidebooks
