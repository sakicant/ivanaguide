Private Šibenik Old Town Walking Tour

Walk Through Šibenik Like a Local

Discover the authentic charm of Šibenik on a private walking tour through its historic Old Town. Wander through narrow stone streets, charming squares, and atmospheric corners while uncovering centuries of history, Venetian influences, and stories that reveal the true spirit of the city.

Led by a licensed local guide born and raised in Šibenik, this experience takes you beyond the pages of guidebooks. Instead of only visiting landmarks, you’ll hear personal memories, local legends, and unique stories passed through generations. The kind of details you can only discover from someone who truly calls Šibenik home.

Why Choose This Private Tour?

  • Private experience tailored to your interests and pace
  • Led by a licensed local guide with personal connection to Šibenik
  • Authentic stories and insights you won’t find in guidebooks
  • Flexible timing with time for photos, and spontaneous discoveries
  • Perfect for couples, solo travelers, and cruise guests

Tour Highlights

  • UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James (entrance not included)
  • Šibenik Old Town and charming stone streets
  • Historic squares and Renaissance architecture
  • St. Barbara Church and City Hall
  • Waterfront views and local atmosphere
  • Stories of Šibenik’s fortresses and their role in the city’s history (fortress visits not included)
