Good to know

Where the Old Town tours start

Obala hrvatske mornarice 1, 22000 Šibenik (next to the INA gas station). Tours run in English. Prices are per group - bring your family or friends at no extra cost, up to the group size listed.

Tours don't include transportation to the meeting point. Need a ride there, to your hotel, or to the airport? Ivana's friend Antonio runs TAXI Antonio, a top-rated local Šibenik taxi service.
