History & Homeland War Private Tour

Discover Šibenik's landmarks and hear a local guide's personal memories of living through the Croatian Homeland War

Experience Šibenik through the stories that shaped the city - from its medieval foundations and Renaissance treasures to the personal memories of those who lived through one of its most challenging chapters.

This private walking tour combines the highlights of Šibenik's historic Old Town with a deeper look into the Croatian Homeland War (1991–1995), offering a unique perspective that connects centuries of history with the experiences of local people. You will explore iconic landmarks, including the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James, while discovering how Šibenik developed, changed, and remained resilient throughout history.

Led by a licensed local guide born and raised in Šibenik, this experience goes beyond traditional sightseeing. Alongside the stories of kings, builders, artists, and the city's cultural heritage, your guide shares her own childhood memories of growing up during the Croatian Homeland War, offering a personal connection to the recent history of Šibenik that cannot be found in guidebooks.

This fully private tour is designed at your pace, allowing time for questions, conversation, photographs, and meaningful moments. It is an authentic way to understand Šibenik not only as a historic city, but as a living place shaped by the stories of its people.

Highlights of the Šibenik History & Homeland War Private Experience

  • Cathedral of St. James - discover Šibenik's UNESCO-listed masterpiece and the story behind one of Croatia's greatest Renaissance achievements. Please note that entrance tickets to the cathedral are not included in the tour price.
  • Šibenik Old Town - walk through centuries of history while exploring stone streets, historic squares, and hidden corners where generations of locals have lived
  • Šibenik Waterfront - learn about the importance of the sea, trade, and the city's strategic position throughout history
  • Šibenik City Hall - admire one of the finest examples of Renaissance architecture and hear stories about the city's political and cultural life
  • St. Barbara Church - discover the church with Šibenik's most unusual clock, said to be the city's hardest clock to read
  • Šibenik Fortresses - hear about the defensive role of the city's fortresses throughout history, including their importance during the Homeland War, while viewing them from the Old Town. Please note that this tour does not include visits to the fortresses themselves.
  • Šibenik During the Homeland War - learn how the city and its people experienced the events of the early 1990s through historical context and the personal memories of your guide

About Your Guide

Your guide is a licensed local expert from Šibenik, officially certified by the Croatian Ministry of Tourism. Born and raised in the city, she combines professional guiding experience with a personal connection to the history she shares.

As a child during the Croatian Homeland War, she experienced the uncertainty and changes that affected everyday life in Šibenik. Through her own memories, as well as stories from family, neighbours, and the local community, she offers a human perspective on this important period of Croatia's recent history.

Her passion is connecting visitors with the real stories behind the places they see - from the craftsmen who built the cathedral centuries ago to the families who lived through the challenges of modern times.

This is more than a sightseeing tour. It is an opportunity to experience Šibenik as a living city - where centuries of heritage, personal memories, and the spirit of its people come together in one unforgettable story.
