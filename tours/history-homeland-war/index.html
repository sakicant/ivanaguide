Šibenik History & Homeland War Private Walking Tour

Discover Šibenik's landmarks and hear a local guide's personal memories of living through the Croatian Homeland War

Discover Šibenik through the stories that shaped the city, from medieval foundations to the personal memories of your guide who lived through its recent history.

This private walking tour combines the highlights of Šibenik’s historic Old Town with a deeper look into the Croatian Homeland War (1991–1995). Explore iconic landmarks such as the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James while discovering how the city developed, changed, and preserved its identity through centuries of challenges.

Led by a licensed local guide born and raised in Šibenik, this experience goes beyond traditional sightseeing. Alongside stories of the city’s heritage, your guide shares personal memories of growing up during the Homeland War, offering a unique perspective that cannot be found in guidebooks.

Why Choose This Private Experience?

  • Private tour tailored to your interests and pace
  • Led by a licensed local guide with a personal connection to Šibenik
  • Hear authentic stories and personal memories
  • Ideal for travelers seeking meaningful experiences beyond standard sightseeing

Tour Highlights

  • UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James (entrance not included)
  • Šibenik Old Town, stone streets, and historic squares
  • Šibenik City Hall and St. Barbara Church
  • Stories of Šibenik’s fortresses and their historical role (fortress visits not included)
  • Šibenik during the Homeland War through local stories and personal memories

What's Included & Not Included

Included

  • Private walking tour with a licensed local guide
  • Historical storytelling combined with personal memories and local insights
  • Authentic stories from a guide with a personal connection to Šibenik’s history
  • Flexible pace adapted to your group
  • Time for questions, photos, and meaningful conversations

Not included

  • Entrance tickets to attractions
  • Transportation to/from the meeting point
  • Food, drinks, and personal expenses
  • Gratuities for the guide (optional and always appreciated)
