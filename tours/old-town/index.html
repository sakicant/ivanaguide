Old Town Private Walking Tour

Walk Through Šibenik Like a Local

Explore the historic heart of Šibenik on a private walking tour through its charming Old Town. This experience takes you beyond the main sights and into the narrow stone streets, hidden corners, and atmospheric squares that reveal the city's true character.

Led by a licensed local guide, you'll discover centuries of history, from medieval foundations to Venetian influences, while learning the stories behind iconic landmarks such as Cathedral of St. James. Along the way, your guide will share personal insights, local legends, and everyday life in Šibenik, giving you a deeper connection to the city.

This fully private tour is designed at your pace, allowing time for questions, photos, and spontaneous discoveries. Whether you're interested in history, architecture, or simply soaking up the atmosphere, this experience offers an authentic way to walk through Šibenik like a local.

Highlights of Šibenik Old Town Private Walking Tour Experience

  • Cathedral of St. James - UNESCO-listed Renaissance landmark and architectural masterpiece. Please note that entrance tickets to the cathedral are not included in the tour price.
  • Šibenik waterfront - scenic promenade perfect for views, cafés, and local life by the sea
  • Šibenik Old Town - labyrinth of stone streets, squares, and authentic Dalmatian atmosphere
  • St. Barbara Church - a small historic church featuring a distinctive clock
  • Šibenik City Hall - elegant Renaissance building overlooking the main square and cathedral
  • Fortresses - iconic hilltop fortresses, viewed from the Old Town while hearing their history and strategic role above the Šibenik. Please note that this tour does not include visits to the fortresses themselves.

About Your Guide

Your guide is a licensed local expert from Šibenik, officially certified by the Croatian Ministry of Tourism. Born and raised in Šibenik, she offers authentic perspectives, personal stories, and local knowledge you won't find in guidebooks.

With years of experience in leading private tours, your guide focuses on creating relaxed, engaging, and fully personalized experiences. Whether it's history, architecture, or local traditions, every walk is adapted to your interests and pace.

This is not just a tour, but a genuine local connection to Šibenik through the eyes of someone who calls it home.
